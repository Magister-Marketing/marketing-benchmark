# Corvane customer policy

In force from Nov 1, 2026.

## Warranty

Every Corvane stick vacuum has a 2-year warranty, except the Aster Pro, which has a
3-year warranty. Registering a vacuum within 30 days of delivery extends the
cover on its motor to 5 years.

## Orders on corvane.example

These terms cover orders placed on corvane.example only.

- Returns: return anything within 60 days of delivery for a full refund. We
  pay for the return shipping.
- Shipping: free shipping on orders over $150. Orders under $150 ship for $9.

## Sold through retailers

The Lumen Lite is sold only through retailers, not on corvane.example. Its
returns and its shipping are the retailer's, and Corvane makes no promise
about either.
