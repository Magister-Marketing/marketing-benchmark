## FAQ

**How long is the free trial?**
Since March every plan comes with a 30-day free trial.

**Is there a free plan?**
No. The trial gives you every feature of Scale instead.

**Which currencies can I invoice in?**
Thirty, including euro, pound and yen.

**Can I set different hourly rates?**
Yes, by person, client or task.

**Which languages is the app in?**
English, German, French and Spanish.

**Do you offer a discount for non-profits?**
Yes, 25% off.

**Can I keep my data when I change plans?**
Yes; nothing is deleted when you move between plans.

**Which payment methods do you accept?**
Cards and bank transfer.

**Can I import time entries from a spreadsheet?**
Yes, from a CSV file in the workspace settings.

**Do you offer onboarding sessions?**
Yes, a one-hour onboarding call for new workspaces.

**Can I rename my workspace?**
Yes, owners can rename it at any time.
