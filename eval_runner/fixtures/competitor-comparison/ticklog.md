## FAQ

**Is the REST API included on Basic?**
Yes. Since October every Ticklog plan includes the REST API.

**Can I get a discount for paying yearly?**
Yes, ask your account manager.

**Can I rename my workspace?**
Yes, owners can rename it at any time.

**Is there a desktop timer?**
Yes, for Windows and macOS.

**How do I close my account?**
Owners can close it from the account page; data is deleted after 30 days.

**Can clients see their own reports?**
Yes, through a read-only link.

**Do you have a referral scheme?**
Yes, a month's credit for every referral.

**Can I set different hourly rates?**
Yes, by person, client or task.

**Which languages is the app in?**
English, German, French and Spanish.

**Do you offer a discount for non-profits?**
Yes, 25% off.
