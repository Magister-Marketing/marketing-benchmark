# Fernhollow Shifts: launch brief

Fernhollow is launching Fernhollow Shifts, a scheduling app for teams paid by the hour,
to three audiences at once. This brief is the source of every fact the copy
may state: do not add others.

## The product

- Build a week's schedule from a saved template.
- Staff swap shifts and pick up open shifts in the app; the manager approves
  each change with one tap.
- Open shifts go out by text, only to staff who are qualified for them and not
  close to overtime.
- Overtime alerts reach the manager before a shift is approved, not after
  payroll runs.
- A labor cost forecast for each location, by week.
- Runs on any phone or browser. No hardware to buy.

## The audiences

Each audience has its own problem and its own proof point, and its copy is
written for that audience alone.

### Restaurants (audience code RST)

Owners and general managers of independent restaurants and small groups, 10 to 60 staff.

- **Problem:** No-shows on the busiest nights leave the floor and the line short, and the manager spends the rush on the phone.
- **Problem keywords:** `no-shows`, `no-show`
- **Proof point:** Kettlewick Kitchens, a group of 4 restaurants, logged 18% less overtime in its first 8 weeks on Fernhollow. Copy quotes it as `18% less overtime`.

### Clinics (audience code CLN)

Practice managers at outpatient clinics (dental, physiotherapy, primary care), front desk and clinical staff.

- **Problem:** The rota lives in a spreadsheet, so staff get double-booked and sessions go uncovered.
- **Problem keywords:** `double-booked`, `double-booking`
- **Proof point:** Practice managers at Northfold Physio got back 5 hours a week of scheduling admin. Copy quotes it as `5 hours a week`.

### Retail (audience code RTL)

Managers of retail chains with 3 to 20 stores and mostly part-time staff.

- **Problem:** Covering the weekend rush in every store at once, with part-timers who change their availability every week.
- **Problem keywords:** `weekend rush`
- **Proof point:** Bramblecote Goods had 98% of shifts filled across its 6 stores last December. Copy quotes it as `98% of shifts filled`.

## Budget

The total launch budget is $80,000.00. Of it, $3,500.00 pays for
creative production. That is not media and gets no rows in the budget. Media
is the rest, split by these four steps, in this order:

1. **Channels.** Google Search 38%, Meta 37%, LinkedIn 25% of media. Email goes to our own
   newsletter list and has no media budget: it gets no rows.
2. **Weeks.** The weights of weeks 1 to 4 are 5, 4, 3, 2. A week's amount is
   the channel's budget times the week's weight, divided by 14, rounded
   down to the cent. Round only at this step.
3. **Remainder.** The cents a channel lost to rounding go to its week 1, so
   its weeks add up to its budget exactly.
4. **Minimum.** No channel-week may be under $4,000.00. One that is
   gets raised to $4,000.00, and the difference is taken from the
   same channel's week 1.
