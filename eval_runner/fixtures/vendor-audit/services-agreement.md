# Services agreement — Northstar Ops × Meridian Media (signed, in effect)

Extracts relevant to monthly billing.

1. **Rates.** Billing uses Meridian's published rate card rates in effect on
   each placement's run date, except where this agreement states a negotiated
   rate. Draft or proposed rates are never billable.
2. **Negotiated rate — newsletter placements.** Newsletter placements are
   billed at a negotiated **$410 per placement**. This supersedes the rate
   card for newsletter placements only.
3. **Volume discount — sponsored articles.** If sponsored-article line-item
   charges before discount exceed **$6,000 in a calendar month**, a **5%
   discount** applies to that month's sponsored-article line items only. The
   discount never applies to other placement types.
4. **Make-goods.** A placement that does not run is not billable. Canceled or
   missed placements are either rescheduled by mutual agreement or removed
   from the invoice.
5. **Delivery log.** Meridian's delivery log is the agreed record of which
   placements ran.
