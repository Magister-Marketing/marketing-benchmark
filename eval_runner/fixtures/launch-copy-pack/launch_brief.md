# Larkspur Halo: launch brief

Larkspur launches four air purifiers on Feb 24, 2027: the Halo Mini, Halo One,
Halo Max and Halo Pet. Each has its own launch price until its own launch
offer ends; the prices and the dates are in claims_register.csv.

What copy may say about a product is decided by the claims register, not by
this brief or the spec sheet. The brief tells you what each product is for.

## Channels

Search ads run on product and category searches. Social ads run in feeds, for each product's buyers below. Emails go to the pre-launch list, people who asked to hear about the launch. SMS goes to the opted-in list on the launch day. Push notifications go to people with the Larkspur app, who own a Breeze, a Drift or an Ember.

## Larkspur Halo Mini (code MINI)

Halo Mini is a small purifier for a desk, a nightstand or a small bedroom. Its SMS link is `lrk.sp/mini`.

- It runs on USB-C power.
- It has a built-in night light.
- Auto mode and a sleep mode.
- There is no app and no Wi-Fi.

Who buys it: renters, students and people who work from a small room, who want cleaner air where they sit or sleep and have no space for a floor unit. They ask whether it is loud at night and whether it needs a socket of its own. Lead with its size for the room it cleans and how quiet it is in sleep mode; the night light is the detail people remember. Do not suggest it is a smaller Halo One: it has its own filter and no app.

## Larkspur Halo One (code ONE)

Halo One is the everyday purifier for bedrooms and living rooms. Its SMS link is `lrk.sp/one`.

- Wi-Fi, with schedules and filter reminders in the Larkspur app.
- A washable pre-filter.
- Auto mode and a sleep mode.

Who buys it: households replacing an old purifier or buying their first, for a bedroom or a living room. They compare room size, noise and running costs, and they like a schedule they can set and forget. Lead with the room size and the sleep mode; the app is the reason to choose it over a cheaper unit. Filter reminders answer the question people ask most after buying.

## Larkspur Halo Max (code MAX)

Halo Max is a purifier for large, open rooms. Its SMS link is `lrk.sp/max`.

- Wi-Fi and the Larkspur app.
- A carbon filter and a washable pre-filter.
- Auto mode and a sleep mode.

Who buys it: owners of open-plan homes, lofts and large living rooms, and small offices. They worry that one unit cannot cover the space. Lead with the room size, then the carbon filter. Smoke is a strong message where people have lived through wildfire seasons, and the register says where it may run.

## Larkspur Halo Pet (code PET)

Halo Pet is a purifier for homes with cats and dogs. Its SMS link is `lrk.sp/pet`.

- A carbon filter for odor.
- A washable pre-filter that catches pet hair.
- Wi-Fi and the Larkspur app.
- Auto mode and a sleep mode.

Who buys it: people with cats or dogs, often more than one, who notice dander, hair and odor. They have usually tried a general purifier. Lead with pet dander and odor; the pre-filter that catches pet hair is the practical detail. Do not make health claims about allergies, however often customers mention them.
