# Proposed marketing actions for review

Forty actions the regional teams have proposed. Each gives the action's date (the day it is first published, sent or goes live), its market and its channel, then what the team wants to do. Nothing beyond what each case says is known about it.

## C01: Clinically proven, dissolving

- Date: 10 September 2026
- Market: US
- Channel: paid social

A paid social ad for Hydra+ Electrolyte Mix with the line "Clinically proven to dissolve fully in 10 seconds", the property the March 2026 laboratory study of the mix measured. No price, no promotion; the ad targets adults aged 25 to 54.

## C02: Spring markdown with free shipping

- Date: 3 March 2026
- Market: US
- Channel: website

The Whey Isolate product page shows "Was $59, now $47" (a 20% markdown) for two weeks, and every order of it ships free during those weeks. The $59 price was charged for 40 days in the six months before. No code, no loyalty discount.

## C03: Comparison with a rival's taste

- Date: 18 May 2026
- Market: UK
- Channel: paid social

A paid social ad for Whey Isolate that says "Smoother than Maxlift Whey", naming the rival brand. The claim is about taste and texture, not price. The ad targets adults.
