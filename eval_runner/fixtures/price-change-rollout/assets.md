## asset-02 (FAQ)

```
## Pricing questions

**How long is the free trial?**
Every new account gets two weeks free, with every feature of the Studio plan switched on. We don't ask for a card until the trial ends.

**What does the Studio plan cost?**
Studio is $49 a month, or $490 a year if you pay for the year up front, and it connects to all 49 integrations. Your booking page, newsletter tool and accounting software stay in step.

**Can I switch plans later?**
Yes. Move between Solo, Studio and Multi-site from Settings at any time. When you move up, we charge only the difference for the rest of your billing period.

**Is there a discount for paying yearly?**
Yes: a year costs the same as ten months. On Solo that is $190 a year instead of $19 a month twelve times.

**Do you charge transaction fees?**
Card payments cost 2.9% plus 25 cents per booking, the same on every plan. Kestrelwick adds nothing on top.
```

## asset-13 (help article)

```
# Changing your plan

You can move between Solo, Studio and Multi-site at any time from Settings, under Billing. This article explains what happens to your bill when you do.

## Moving up

When you move from Solo to Studio, you pay the difference for the rest of your current billing period, and $49 per month from your next bill.

## Moving down

A move down takes effect at the end of your billing period, and nothing is refunded for time already paid. If you move from Studio to Solo, Studio's staff logins and waitlists are switched off, and StudioSync keeps working for the account owner only.

## Paying yearly

Yearly plans cost the same as ten months: $190 per year for Solo, $490 per year for Studio and $990 per year for Multi-site. You can switch to yearly billing at any time, and we credit what is left of your current month.

## Still in your trial?

Changing plans during your 14-day trial is free, and your trial's end date stays the same.

If anything on your bill looks wrong, write to billing@kestrelwick.example and we will answer within one working day.
```
