# Wrenfold Home: warranty policy

Every product carries the warranty of its category, below, unless its row in
product_specs.csv gives a number in `warranty_years`: that number is the
product's warranty instead, longer or shorter. A product whose category has
none and whose row gives none has no warranty.

| category | warranty |
|---|---|
| desk | 5 years |
| bookcase | 5 years |
| side table | 2 years |
| floor lamp | 2 years |
| table lamp | 2 years |
| dining chair | 2 years |
| armchair | 2 years |
| sofa | 10 years |
| sideboard | 2 years |
| media unit | 2 years |
| wall shelf | 2 years |
| bed frame | 10 years |
| headboard | 2 years |
| cushion | none |
| storage basket | none |
| laundry hamper | none |
| planter | none |
