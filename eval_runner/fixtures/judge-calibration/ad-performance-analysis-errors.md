## 2. Best and Worst Performing Campaigns

### Best Performing: `Prospecting - Small-Space Interest`
- **Highest ROAS & Lowest CPA:** Delivered a **3.91x ROAS** and **$17.39 CPA** (only 25.6% of the $68 unit price), preserving healthy product margin.
- **Flawless Delivery Stability:** While every other campaign degraded, Small-Space Interest maintained perfectly flat performance across all four weeks:
  - Weekly conversions: 34 → 35 → 34 → 35.
  - CTR was rock-solid at **1.90%** every week (1,520 → 1,560 → 1,540 → 1,580 clicks).
  - Conversion rate held steady between **2.21% and 2.24%**.
  - Weekly frequency barely moved (1.8 → 2.1), indicating an engaged niche audience that is not yet fatigued.

### Worst Performing: `Launch Teaser - Video Views` (and the Dollar Drag of `Broad`)
- **Worst Unit Economics (`Launch Teaser - Video Views`):** Deeply unprofitable. At **0.48x–0.63x ROAS**, it burns $0.37 to $0.52 of every dollar spent. Its CPA of **$107.14–$142.86** is substantially higher than the retail price of the lamp itself ($68.00). Optimizing for video views buys passive scrollers, not buyers.
- **Worst Dollar Bleed (`Prospecting - Broad`):** While Teaser had the worst ratios, Broad represented 49% of total spend ($4,800). By Week 4, its CPA spiked to **$48.00** and ROAS plummeted to **1.42x** (producing only 25 purchases vs. 60 in Week 1). It is the primary cause of account profit erosion.

## 3. Week-over-Week Trend Diagnosis: Acute Creative & Audience Fatigue

The most critical trend problem in the account is **severe creative and audience fatigue** across `Prospecting - Broad` and `Retargeting - 30d Engagers`.

When ads are left unrefreshed, Meta’s algorithm re-serves the same assets to the same users, initiating a classic decay spiral:

$$\text{Frequency} \uparrow \longrightarrow \text{CTR} \downarrow \longrightarrow \text{CPC} \uparrow \longrightarrow \text{CVR} \downarrow \longrightarrow \text{CPA} \uparrow \text{ and } \text{ROAS} \downarrow$$

### Concrete Week-over-Week Degradation

1. **Prospecting - Broad (Creative Burnout):**
   - **Weekly Frequency climbed 65%:** 2.0 → 2.4 → 2.9 → **3.3**. For top-of-funnel broad prospecting, a weekly frequency of 3.3 means users are seeing the exact same ad every 2 days.
   - **CTR halved (-50%):** 1.80% → 1.50% → 1.20% → **0.90%**. Users are tuning the creative out.
   - **CPC climbed 76%:** $0.33 → $0.38 → $0.45 → **$0.58**.
   - **On-site CVR fell 28%:** 1.67% → 1.52% → 1.36% → **1.21%**.
   - **Financial Impact:** Weekly conversions collapsed from 60 down to 25 (-58.3%), CPA surged from **$20.00 to $48.00** (+140%), and ROAS sank from **3.40x to 1.42x**.

2. **Retargeting - 30d Engagers (Extreme Audience Saturation):**
   - **Weekly Frequency nearly doubled:** 6.1 → 7.8 → 9.6 → **11.4**. In Week 4, users in this custom audience saw the ad ~1.6 times per day.
   - **CTR fell 26%:** 3.00% → 2.81% → 2.50% → **2.21%**.
   - **On-site CVR fell 34%:** 2.89% → 2.76% → 2.38% → **1.92%**.
   - **Financial Impact:** Weekly conversions dropped from 26 to 14 (-46.2%), CPA climbed from **$15.38 to $28.57** (+85.8%), and ROAS fell from **4.42x to 2.38x**. The retargeting audience is tiny (implied reach fell from 4,918 to 2,895 people) and heavily over-budgeted.

## 5. Data Limitations & Data Quality Anomalies

### Most Significant Data Limitation
**Single-Channel Meta In-Platform Modeled Attribution with Missing Window Context:**
- We are looking entirely at Meta’s self-reported attribution (typically default 7-day click / 1-day view). Meta routinely takes credit for view-through purchases that would have occurred via organic search or email, especially on retargeting.
- Without GA4, server-side CAPI event deduplication, or post-click incrementality test data (such as a geo-holdout), we cannot verify whether Retargeting’s 83 conversions were genuinely incremental or simply captured existing brand momentum.

### Notable Data Quality Anomalies in the Fixture
1. **Perfect Synthetic Average Order Value ($68.0000 across all 397 purchases):**
   - In every row where conversions and revenue are present, $\frac{\text{revenue}}{\text{conversions}} = \mathbf{\$68.00}$ exactly (e.g., $\$4,080 / 60 = \$68.00$; $\$2,380 / 35 = \$68.00$; $\$1,768 / 26 = \$68.00$; $\$204 / 3 = \$68.00$).
   - Real DTC eCommerce involves multi-unit carts, shipping fees, sales taxes, or discount codes. This exact 1:1 unit-to-order ratio confirms the fixture’s synthetic nature.
2. **Missing Week 3 Row Data for Launch Teaser:**
   - In row `Launch Teaser - Video Views` for week `2026-07-20`, conversions and revenue are completely blank (`,,`), despite recording 455 clicks and $250 spend. This indicates an unannounced tracking dropout rather than a confirmed zero.
3. **Rigid Weekly Budgets:**
   - Spend is locked at exact static numbers ($1,200, $600, $400, $250) across all four weeks with zero day-to-day bidding fluctuation.
