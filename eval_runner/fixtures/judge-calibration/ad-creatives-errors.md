Here are three genuinely different Meta feed ad concepts for the **Arc Clip Lamp**, crafted specifically for internal review. Each targets a distinct audience motivation (reclaiming physical workspace, day-to-night cordless mobility, and precision lighting for detailed craft work) while strictly adhering to Cedar & Coil’s approved voice and authoritative product facts.

---

### Concept 1: The Workspace Reclaim (Desk Footprint Angle)

* **Audience insight:** Compact-desk workers and urban apartment dwellers constantly battle surface clutter. Standard desk lamp bases consume prime real estate next to laptops and notebooks, forcing an annoying compromise between good lighting and usable work area.
* **Visual direction:** Static high-angle eye-level shot of a compact studio desk. In frame: a 13-inch laptop, open notebook, and pen. The Arc Clip Lamp is clamped securely to the desk edge (showing the padded clamp protecting the finished wood), arching focused illumination directly over the workspace without any base resting on the desk surface. Warm, natural morning ambiance—clean, practical, and unpretentious.
* **Primary text:** Small desks shouldn’t force a choice between a lamp base and your notebook. The Arc Clip Lamp clamps to edges up to 2.25 inches thick, casting warm, focused task light right where you work—without taking up an inch of desktop.
* **Headline:** Task lighting that frees your desk.
* **Description:** 3 brightness levels. Clamps anywhere.
* **CTA:** Explore the Arc Clip Lamp
* **Test hypothesis:** Concept 1 will achieve a higher click-through rate (CTR) and lower cost per click (CPC) than Concept 2 among self-identified remote workers and small-space dwellers, because eliminating desk clutter addresses their most immediate physical workday friction.

### Concept 2: Day-to-Night Mobility (Portable Routine Angle)

* **Audience insight:** Renters and students in shared flats or studios lead modular lives where one room serves as study, living area, and bedroom. Rather than buying multiple bulky lamps, they value thoughtful, portable lighting that moves easily through their day without damaging rental furniture.
* **Visual direction:** Two-panel lifestyle sequence showing a calm evening transition. Panel 1: Clamped to a narrow wooden shelf above a reading chair. Panel 2: The same lamp clamped to a slim bedside frame emitting a gentle, warm glow, completely cordless. The included canvas storage sleeve rests neatly on a bedside ledge. Golden, calming evening tones.
* **Primary text:** Work at your desk, read on the couch, wind down in bed. With up to 8 hours of battery on the lowest brightness and a padded clamp that won't mark furniture, the Arc Clip Lamp moves wherever you need calm, adaptable light.
* **Headline:** Moves from desk to bedside.
* **Description:** Cordless, rechargeable light.
* **CTA:** Explore the Arc Clip Lamp
* **Test hypothesis:** Concept 2 will outperform Concept 3 on link CTR across a broad renter and student audience, because demonstrating versatile day-to-night lifestyle portability provides broader everyday utility than specialized maker task lighting.

---

### Concept 3: Precision Detail & Color Tone (Maker & Craft Angle)

* **Audience insight:** Makers, artists, and crafters working on intricate projects in compact nooks require flexible, glare-free task lighting with adjustable color temperatures. Fixed overhead fixtures cast shadows and strain eyes during delicate evening creative sessions.
* **Visual direction:** Intimate, tactile close-up of hands carving a linocut print block or sketching on a drafting table in a small studio corner. The Arc Clip Lamp is clamped securely to an adjacent shelf, angled closely over the workpiece. The lighting is crisp and even, highlighting both the lamp's clean matte hardware and the rich texture of the craft materials.
* **Primary text:** Fine work needs precise light, even in tight quarters. The Arc Clip Lamp offers 3 brightness levels and 2 color temperatures, delivering focused, glare-free illumination to your project bench, drafting table, or shelf.
* **Headline:** Adjustable light for close-up work.
* **Description:** 2 color temps & 3 brightness modes.
* **CTA:** Explore the Arc Clip Lamp
* **Test hypothesis:** Concept 3 will generate a higher thumb-stop rate (3-second video/rich engagement) and outbound CTR among craft and maker interest clusters compared to Concept 1, because showing tactile craft execution and color-temperature control directly signals specialized functional utility.
