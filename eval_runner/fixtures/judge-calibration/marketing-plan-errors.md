# Northstar Ops — 90-Day Go-To-Market Marketing Plan

---

## (1) ICP and Positioning

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
* **Company Profile:** Growing B2B tech and service teams (20–150 employees) experiencing operational coordination friction across cross-functional departments (e.g., Product, Engineering, Customer Onboarding, Implementation, and Client Services).
* **Target Buyer Persona:** Head of Operations, VP of Operations, Director of Product Operations, or Chief of Staff accountable for operational cadence, inter-team handoff velocity, and eliminating organizational bottlenecks.
* **Target User Persona:** Operations managers, cross-functional leads, and project leads who execute and oversee multi-team deliverables.
* **Core Operational Pains:**
  * Multi-step handoffs between departments stall in opaque backlogs (e.g., Sales to Customer Success, Product to Implementation).
  * Leadership attempts to patch visibility gaps by scheduling recurring weekly status and alignment meetings.
  * Team members suffer from meeting fatigue and coordination drag while cross-functional handoff status remains unclear.
* **Core Desires:**
  * Asynchronous, self-evident visibility into cross-team workflows without manual follow-up.
  * Fast, structured handoffs with clear accountability.
  * Reclaiming calendar hours by eliminating low-value recurring status meetings.

### Approved Positioning
* **Core Value Proposition:** *Faster, visible handoffs without adding another weekly status meeting.*
* **Strategic Category Framing:** Asynchronous Operations Workspace for growing teams.
* **Differentiation:** Unlike traditional documentation wikis or passive project management task boards that still rely on weekly meetings to explain status, Northstar Ops connects cross-functional handoffs directly into structured, visible workflows that make operational progress obvious across teams.

## (2) Channel Plan

To maximize the impact of the $15,000 budget over 90 days, Northstar Ops concentrates resources on **exactly three channels**:

### Selected Channels & Rationales

1. **Targeted Paid Search (Google Ads — High-Intent Core Search)**
   * *Rationale:* Captures immediate in-market demand from operational buyers actively searching for solutions to handoff bottlenecks, workflow automation, and cross-team coordination (e.g., "cross-functional workflow software", "operations handoff tool", "asynchronous project handoff"). This provides fast, quantifiable feedback on messaging and trial acquisition.
2. **Organic Content Strategy & Targeted SEO (Bottom-of-Funnel & Mid-Funnel)**
   * *Rationale:* Builds durable compounding inbound authority around core operational problems: operational handoff frameworks, status meeting audits, and asynchronous operations checklists. High-utility templates and guides drive qualified organic traffic and directly support product-led onboarding.
3. **Targeted Paid Social (LinkedIn Sponsored Content)**
   * *Rationale:* Provides surgical B2B demographic targeting by exact job titles (Heads of Operations, Chiefs of Staff, Ops Directors) at companies with 20–150 employees. Allows Northstar Ops to deliver problem-aware creative and visual product demonstrations directly to target decision-makers feeling the pain of recurring status meetings.

### Excluded Channels & Rationales
* **Cold Email Outbound / SDR Motion:** Excluded because building out dedicated outbound infrastructure (cold domain reputation, email enrichment tools, verification software, SDR tooling) would consume a significant portion of the $15,000 budget without sufficient run-rate to test and optimize messaging.
* **Broad Consumer & Social Channels (Meta, TikTok, X, YouTube):** Excluded because broad ad networks suffer from high impression waste for niche B2B operations software, driving up Cost Per Qualified Lead compared to intent-driven and title-targeted platforms.
* **Affiliate, Creator & Influencer Programs:** Excluded because the product is in its initial GTM phase; affiliate programs require established baseline conversion rates, mature brand equity, and proven referral economics to be viable.
* **Event Sponsorships & Field Marketing:** Excluded due to prohibitive fixed costs (booth fees, travel, printed materials) that would exhaust the total $15,000 budget on one or two unproven offline events.

## (4) Funnel and Activation Metrics

### Documented Trial Activation Event
* **Activation Milestone:** A trial team **invites at least two teammates (≥2 invites accepted)** AND **completes at least one shared workflow** within the 14-day trial period.
* **Cohort Closed-Window Rule:** Activation rates are measured exclusively against closed 14-day cohorts. Workspaces still within their active 14-day trial are tracked as *Pending* to prevent premature denominator inflation.

### Full-Funnel Metric Structure

| Funnel Stage | Primary Metric | Operational Calculation / Definition | Leading Indicator |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Acquisition** | Qualified Unique Visitors & Trial Starts | Total unique visitors to high-intent landing pages; total 14-day self-serve trial accounts created. | Paid search CTR; Landing page conversion rate (Visitor-to-Trial). |
| **2. Onboarding** | First Workspace Setup Rate | % of trial accounts that configure an operational workspace or import a template within 24 hours. | Time to first workspace creation; Template selection rate. |
| **3. Teammate Invites** | Teammate Invitation Rate | % of trial accounts sending $\ge 2$ teammate invites within 72 hours of signup. | In-app invite prompt view-to-send rate; Team member acceptance rate. |
| **4. Core Activation** | **Trial Team Activation Rate** *(Key North-Star)* | $\frac{\text{Closed Trial Accounts with } \ge 2 \text{ Teammates Joined AND } \ge 1 \text{ Completed Shared Workflow}}{\text{Total Closed 14-Day Trial Cohorts}}$ | In-app workflow initiation; Task handoff execution between teammates; Days to first handoff. |
| **5. Trial-to-Paid** | Paid Conversion Rate | % of activated 14-day trial teams upgrading to an active paid subscription plan. | Daily Active Teammates (DAT); Volume of completed shared workflows in Week 2. |

## (7) Top Three Risks and Mitigations

### Risk 1: The "Single-Player Trap" (Failure to Invite Teammates)
* **Risk Description:** The initial user (e.g., Head of Operations) signs up and evaluates Northstar Ops as a solo user, treating it as an individual task board rather than an inter-team workspace, preventing the team from meeting the activation milestone (inviting $\ge 2$ teammates).
* **Root Cause:** Onboarding flows that allow a solo user to complete setup without experiencing the multi-player value of cross-functional handoffs.
* **Mitigation Strategy:**
  1. Restructure onboarding with an explicit "Invite Your Cross-Functional Partner" step required to unlock pre-built workflow templates.
  2. Implement an interactive "Simulated Teammate Handoff" sandbox so solo evaluators can test handoff triggers and visible status notifications before inviting real colleagues.
  3. Deploy behavioral lifecycle emails on Day 2 and Day 4 highlighting how peers use asynchronous handoffs to cut status meetings.

### Risk 2: High Paid Acquisition Costs Burning the Limited Budget
* **Risk Description:** Highly competitive B2B SaaS search terms on Google Ads and elevated CPMs on LinkedIn Ads inflate Cost Per Click (CPC) and Cost Per Trial (CPT), exhausting the $15,000 budget before achieving statistical significance on trial conversion.
* **Root Cause:** Bidding on broad, competitive category keywords (e.g., "project management software") or targeting overly broad audience segments.
* **Mitigation Strategy:**
  1. Restrict Google Ads campaigns exclusively to exact-match and phrase-match long-tail, high-intent keywords focused on operations friction (e.g., "asynchronous workflow tool", "cross-functional handoff software").
  2. Enforce negative keyword lists excluding generic productivity, task management, and consumer terms.
  3. On LinkedIn, strictly limit audience targeting to verified operations titles at 20–150 employee companies, set daily spend caps ($60/day), and trigger automated pause rules if Cost Per Trial exceeds target thresholds.

### Risk 3: Low Trial Workflow Completion (Friction at First Handoff)
* **Risk Description:** Invited teammates join the workspace but fail to complete a shared workflow within the 14-day trial window, falling short of the second required activation condition.
* **Root Cause:** Empty-state friction—teams face a blank canvas and are unsure how to configure handoff triggers and dependencies.
* **Mitigation Strategy:**
  1. Default new trial accounts to pre-populated, ready-to-run workflow templates (e.g., *Customer Implementation Handoff*, *Product-to-Ops Launch Handoff*).
  2. Provide clear visual step markers highlighting when a handoff is waiting for review.
  3. Send real-time asynchronous notifications to assignees with a one-click action: *"Handoff received from [Name] — click here to accept without a meeting."*
