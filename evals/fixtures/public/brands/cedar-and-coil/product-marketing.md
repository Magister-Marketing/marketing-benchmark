# Cedar & Coil

Cedar & Coil is a fictional direct-to-consumer brand for people who work or
make things in compact spaces. Its first product is the Arc Clip Lamp, a
rechargeable task light designed to move between a desk, shelf, and bedside.

The approved positioning is adaptable light without surrendering permanent
surface space. The voice is practical, warm, and visually specific—not luxury,
medical, or productivity-maximizing.

All campaign work must remain draft-only. Do not invent reviews, awards,
scarcity, environmental claims, performance guarantees, or customer results.
