# Harborline Sleep Education

Harborline Sleep Education is a fictional educational coaching service for
adults who want a more consistent bedtime routine. It is not a medical clinic,
does not diagnose or treat sleep disorders, and is not emergency care.

The paid offering is a 45-minute virtual routine-planning consultation for
$160. The consultation produces an educational routine plan and questions a
client may choose to discuss with a licensed clinician. Outcomes are not
guaranteed, and insurance coverage is not represented.
