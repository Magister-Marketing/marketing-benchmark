# Ad creative

Read the supplied brand facts before drafting. Create genuinely different
angles based on distinct audience motivations, not paraphrases of one idea.
For each concept, label the copy, visual direction, audience insight, and test
hypothesis. Respect every channel limit in the task. Never invent proof,
customers, performance, scarcity, or product capabilities. Keep all work in a
reviewable draft; do not create, fund, publish, or activate a campaign.
