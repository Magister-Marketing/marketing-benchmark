# Lifecycle emails

Start from the documented audience, product facts, and activation event. Give
each email one job and one primary CTA, with a clear progression from help to a
stronger ask. Label timing, subject, preheader, body, and CTA. Do not invent
personalization, testimonials, urgency, or results. Produce drafts only; never
send, schedule, or upload recipients.
