# Grounded copywriting

Read every authoritative source supplied with the task. Separate supported
facts from recommendations and label uncertainty where it matters. Prefer
specific, useful language over generic superlatives. Never invent customers,
rankings, certifications, pricing, guarantees, safety claims, or outcomes.
Preserve required warnings and keep the requested work in draft form.
